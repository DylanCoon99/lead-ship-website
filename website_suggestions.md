**TrueModel™ AI Verification**

**Eliminate False Positives Before They Reach Your CRM**

Most lead validation platforms verify contact information.

Lead-Ship goes significantly further.

TrueModel™ Verification evaluates a prospect's actual business model using live operational, regulatory, technographic, and firmographic intelligence—not simply self-reported industry classifications.

If a prospect doesn't possess the infrastructure, technologies, licenses, operational footprint, or business characteristics required by your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), the lead is automatically rejected before it ever reaches your CRM.

Instead of relying solely on SIC or NAICS codes, TrueModel™ determines whether a company truly fits your campaign based on real-world operational data.

Lead-Ship manages every stage of the demand generation process from beginning to end, eliminating the need for multiple software platforms and manual processes.
