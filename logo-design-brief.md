# Lead Ship — Logo Design Brief

Use this file to generate a new logo for the Lead Ship website. The logo should feel native to the website's visual identity described below.

---

## Brand Overview

**Company Name:** Lead Ship (stylized as "Lead Ship" or "Lead-Ship")
**Tagline:** The End-to-End AI-Powered Marketing Operations Platform for B2B Demand Generation
**Short tagline options:** "AI-Powered MarOps" or "Request | Validate | Deliver"

Lead Ship is a B2B SaaS platform that manages the entire demand generation campaign lifecycle — from campaign requests and quoting through AI-powered lead validation, routing, reporting, and billing. The platform replaces multiple disconnected marketing operations tools with one unified, AI-powered solution.

---

## Existing Logo (Reference)

The current logo (lead-ship-logo-v1.png, attached) features:
- A circle outline with three arrows diverging outward from a center point (pointing up-left, left, and down-left)
- The arrows are in three colors: dark navy blue, teal/cyan, and green
- "LEAD-SHIP" in bold dark text to the right of the icon
- "ROUTE | VALIDATE | DELIVER" as a smaller tagline underneath
- Clean, modern, corporate feel

**What to keep from this logo:** The multi-directional arrow concept (representing routing/delivery/distribution of leads), the clean corporate aesthetic, and the general idea of movement and direction.

**What to update:** The color palette should match the website's theme (see below). The tagline could be updated to reflect the new AI-powered positioning. The overall style should feel more premium and modern to match the website's polished look.

## Typography

- **Font Family:** Inter (Google Fonts) — a clean, modern sans-serif
- **Headings:** Font weights 700 (bold) and 800 (extrabold), tight tracking
- **Logo text** on the website currently uses: `text-xl font-bold` (Inter Bold, ~20px)
- The overall typographic feel is clean, geometric, and highly legible

---

## Visual Style & Design Language

- **Overall aesthetic:** Clean, modern, professional SaaS. Not playful — polished and enterprise-ready but not stuffy.
- **Backgrounds:** Dark navy gradients (`from-navy-950 via-navy-900 to-navy-800`) for hero sections and CTAs, alternating white (`#fff`) and surface (`#f8fafc`) for content sections.
- **Subtle texture:** Dot-grid pattern overlaid on dark sections at very low opacity (5-7%), giving a technical/blueprint feel.
- **Cards:** White backgrounds with `border border-gray-100`, slight `rounded-2xl` corners, subtle shadows on hover.
- **Icons:** Heroicons outline style (1.5px stroke weight), used inside small colored background squares (`w-10 h-10 bg-accent/10 rounded-lg`).
- **Buttons:** Solid accent blue (`#2563eb`) with white text, rounded-lg, medium padding. Secondary buttons use bordered/outline style.
- **Shapes:** Rounded corners throughout (lg to 2xl). No sharp edges.
- **Motion:** Subtle fade-up animations on scroll. Nothing aggressive.

---

## Logo Requirements

1. **Icon mark:** Should work as a standalone icon (for favicon, app icon, social media avatar) at small sizes (32x32, 64x64). The current arrow-in-circle concept works well — refine it to use the website's navy/blue palette.

2. **Full logo (icon + wordmark):** Horizontal layout with the icon on the left and "Lead Ship" text on the right. Should work on both dark (navy) and light (white) backgrounds.

3. **Color usage in the logo:**
   - Primary icon colors should use Navy 950 (`#0a1628`) and Accent Blue (`#2563eb`)
   - Can incorporate Accent Light (`#3b82f6`) as a secondary/gradient tone
   - Avoid using the emerald/purple/amber/rose feature colors in the logo — keep it to navy + blue
   - Must look good on both white and dark navy backgrounds (provide both versions)

4. **Style:** Clean, geometric, modern. Consistent with the Inter font aesthetic. No gradients in the icon unless very subtle. Flat or near-flat design.

5. **Tagline version:** Optionally include a version with a tagline underneath. Suggested taglines:
   - "AI-Powered Marketing Operations"
   - "Request | Validate | Deliver"

---

## Where the Logo Will Be Used

- **Navbar:** Small icon (32x32 rounded square) + "Lead Ship" text, displayed on dark navy transparent/blurred backgrounds
- **Footer:** Same icon + text on dark navy (`#0a1628`) background
- **Favicon:** Just the icon mark, 32x32 and 16x16
- **Open Graph / Social:** Full logo on white or navy background, 1200x630
- **Marketing materials:** Slide decks, email headers, etc.

---

## Summary

Create a modern, clean logo for Lead Ship that uses the navy (`#0a1628`) and blue (`#2563eb`, `#3b82f6`) color palette, feels at home on the website's polished dark-gradient and white section design, and retains the directional/routing arrow concept from the existing logo. The result should feel premium, enterprise-ready, and distinctly AI/tech — not generic or clip-art-like.
